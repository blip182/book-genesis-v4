## Back cover

Most of us are handed two options in life. We have a choice to take life as it is, or reject it and start over.

Jena Crossland Brooks has experienced what it was like to only see those two paths, until she built the third and made it into a life that fits her own terms.

The Third Option is the method she built from her own life, and a guide on how to implement it in your own.

**You will learn to:**

- Write your own definition of a life that fits
- Make a counter instead of a yes or a no
- Start before you feel ready
- Keep score on a board you chose
- Run the Fit Audit on anything, from a career move to a Tuesday

## About the Author (interior page, also lives in the manuscript)

Jena Crossland Brooks is a wife, mom, and careerist who refused to accept the options put in front of her. Growing up, she knew that she never fit the molds society had to offer, so she set out to build a life that fits her terms.

She has spent time exploring careers, hobbies, motherhood, failures, and relationships to discover what truly matters to her and what deserves her energy.

As someone who thrives in connecting creativity, strategy, and operations, she decided to write down the method that she has been living before even knowing the language. The Third Option is shared in hopes of empowering you to start where you are and choose to create, build, and own your life.

It's time to stop accepting what's offered to you and start activating the option that others don't look for.
